HANZI WRITING — THIRD-PARTY DATA ATTRIBUTION

HSK 3.0 VOCABULARY
HSK 3.0 word list, all levels by Gushi
https://gushichinese.com/hsk-3.0/downloads
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The source is the official HSK 3.0 examination syllabus published in November 2025.
The source dataset separates advanced entries into levels 7, 8 and 9 for its own
learning interface; this app combines those entries into the syllabus's shared
Level 7–9 advanced band. Changes made for this app: conversion to indexed SQLite,
merging those three subdivisions into the published shared band, and adding
Traditional-form mappings from the credited CEDICT/TOCFL sources where available.

TOCFL VOCABULARY
Vocabulary data from LinZi — https://www.linzi.app/data/
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
The data is based on the TOCFL 華語八千詞 vocabulary syllabus and includes
Novice 1, Novice 2, and Levels 1–5.

CC-CEDICT
CC-CEDICT Chinese-English dictionary data and contributors
https://www.mdbg.net/chinese/dictionary?page=cc-cedict
License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
The dictionary data is converted into the app's SQLite database and may be
normalized during import.
